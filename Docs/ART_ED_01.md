# ART-ED-01 — Warden escort drone (visual trial)

Working name, not accepted canon. ED01D is a fictional Dominion twin-rotor escort drone, smaller than Watcher (16.60 vs 25 units span), 11.77 units long including guns at rest, Y-up/+Z forward, 1740 rendered triangles. Compared with ED01C, the main body is wider and longer, the hollow ducted rotors smaller and more faceted, and the forward turret bases are integrated into raised shoulders. Existing mesh groups, prefab IDs and materials remain; yaw and aft sockets are repositioned, pitch and muzzle local transforms unchanged. ED02A separates the existing rotor blades into independent local-Y pivots and animates them in opposite directions. An opt-in Sandbox-only preview follows a tangent circle at its authored altitude; the reusable prefab keeps the preview disabled. Collision, damage, weapons and mission integration are deferred. Each turret must ultimately aim independently of patrol heading; verify usable yaw/pitch arcs and body clearance before enabling fire. No unrestricted traverse is promised.

Prefab: `Assets/_Project/Prefabs/Aircraft/ED01_Warden.prefab`. Drag into CombatSandbox or Mission01 temporarily at cruise Y78, scale 1, near the airborne player path. Review in actual game camera; remove temporary scene instance before saving. Compare with Watcher and Tiger. Inspect all mesh/material references and rotate each yaw/pitch pivot in prefab mode (undo changes after inspection). Do not create a new mission scene in this atom.

Reproducible source: `Tools/Blender/build_ed01_warden.py`. OBJ files are included. To save an editable `.blend` from the repo root use Blender's bundled Python via `blender --background --factory-startup --python Tools/Blender/build_ed01_warden.py`. Geometry export validates source solids' winding, closure and nonzero triangles. Unity import and Blender execution still need local validation.

ED02A test: open CombatSandbox and press Play. The existing ED01_Warden circles from its authored pose at 24 units/s, radius 65, with opposite rotor rotation (840 degrees/s). R resets its pose and blade phase. Tune on the scene instance only. The two independent turret hierarchies and muzzle sockets are unchanged; no targeting or firing yet. Check that no fixed spokes or shrouds rotate and all mesh references remain valid. If comparing the static silhouette, disable WardenPatrolPreview on the instance. Do not apply its enabled override to the reusable prefab.
