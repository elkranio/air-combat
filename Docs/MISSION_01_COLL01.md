# COLL-01 — visual aircraft separation v0

Mission01-only experiment. In normal flight Lynx visually rises over nearby living air targets, including Watcher and Overlord. At 56 units the proximity envelope begins, reaching full 16-unit lift at 23 units; relative motion predicts up to .35 seconds ahead to handle head-on passes. Smooth rise (.10 s damping) and return (.25 s damping); take the largest requested lift across contacts, never sum. No damage, forced steering, speed, fuel, camera, radar, weapon origin or root/collider changes. Static scene contacts are cached once. A separate model branch and engine sockets move in world-up; muzzle sockets remain at the gameplay altitude. Shadows follow the moved mesh naturally. Destroyed/escaped targets stop contributing. R, crash, boundary failure, landing and disable clear the offset; Tab freezes it with the sortie.

Open Mission01_FirstSortie and Play; no authoring menu or scene rebuild required. Launch sequence Inspector has Visual Air Separation (toggle), Air Separation Lift, Start Distance and Full Distance. Test direct head-on/crossing passes, following a close target, boosting and firing while raised, multiple nearby targets, Tab and R, and ensure no lift at distant passes or on landing. Disable the toggle for an A/B comparison. If the expected Lynx model branch is missing or has colliders, the helper logs a warning and leaves gameplay untouched. This is cosmetic: shots and incoming damage still use the original gameplay plane, so visual muzzle/tracer separation during overlap is a known tradeoff. No sound or camera shake yet. Unity compile and Game view remain the acceptance gate.

## COLL-02 — emergency evasion

Supersedes the proximity lift above. Predict closest approach from relative XZ velocity (0.45 s horizon), with a 23-unit danger radius and a swept-crossing fallback. A qualifying contact triggers one fixed 1.0-second visual maneuver: .35 s climb, .20 s crest, .45 s descent. Height follows smoothstep, pitch follows the derivative of height (capped at 25 degrees nose-up / 20 nose-down); the model and exhaust sockets rotate about the aircraft pivot. The 16-unit lift and Inspector toggle remain; the old start distance now rearms the contact once it clears 56 units. A parallel or receding contact alone does not trigger evasion. The helper is still mission-local and render-only; slow prolonged overlap or very late detection may need tuning.

During the maneuver inhibit the player trigger before its LateUpdate, cancel any queued cannon burst via CeaseFire, and retain the normal cooldown. Existing rounds remain live. Held LMB resumes normal fire only after evasion and cooldown. Movement, yaw, speed, boost, damage, terrain collision and camera remain unchanged. R, crash, landing, failure, player death, disable and turning the feature off clear the pose and inhibit; Tab pauses the sequence. Test head-on, crossing, overtaking, near-parallel and repeated approaches, hold LMB through evasion, confirm the burst cancels and the cooldown is not restarted each frame. Check reset/Tab and both nozzle poses. No scene changes. Unity runtime validation required.
