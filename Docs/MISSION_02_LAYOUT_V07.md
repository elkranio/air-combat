# M02 V07 — Open-ended canyons

Requires the installed V06 scene and CA07/CA07B. Open and save M02, run **Air Combat > Missions > M02 - Apply Open Canyons V07**. This replaces only the two V06 massif objects with four closed meshes, retains the two V04 massifs, and bakes the same tactical map asset at Y78. Old V06 assets remain available for rollback/history. Scene and cargo path edits are grouped under Undo; on failure the installer rolls back and attempts to restore the previous map bake. Do not run the historical CA07B path menu after V07; it would restore the superseded V06 routes.

C1 stays at (-2130, 460) and flies directly from (-2850, 613), 12 degrees north of west. C2 stays at (1510, -2200) and flies directly from (1758, -3065), 16 degrees east of south. Both routes are flat Y78 and end 420 units beyond the respective operation boundary. New canyon cuts are irregular and asymmetric, preserve the existing player-facing entrances, and leave the pads, guides, main base, convoy and operation bounds alone. The cut source meshes have 576 triangles total; closed directed-edge incidence and constrained triangulation area checked offline. Minimum centerline clearance to the cut footprints is 90/91 units, routes are 736/900 units long. Other retained massifs are checked conservatively against expanded renderer bounds by the installer, along with terrain and new footprints. No height overflight needed.

Existing CA07B acceleration, HP, dispatch and reserve-delay settings are unchanged. Cargo yaw follows the new first segment (required for socket alignment). Actual escape deadlines change because the routes are shorter. Retune only after local acceptance. Test C1/C2 individually, including ground cargo abort, loaded escape and R; then Full Sortie. Confirm Tab map shows both cuts, radar/flight collision follows the new walls, and old player routes remain flyable. Unity compilation, map bake and gameplay checks are local gates.
