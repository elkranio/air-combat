# MAP-01 — static tactical geography

Base `3d57ddb`. Open the saved mission in Edit Mode. **Air Combat > Missions > Bake Mission Map** opens a preview window. Ground bounds and airfield/runway are preselected for Mission01 by hierarchy name; any other scene can supply its own renderer and optional transform. Cruise Y comes from the launch sequence, or can be entered explicitly. Press Bake / refresh static map. Review the north-up preview and commit the generated asset plus all new .meta files. Re-bake after editing the terrain/layout or altitude. No scene edits.

The ground renderer's world X/Z bounds define the map rectangle. `TerrainSectionUnion` reuses the exact existing radar scan sweep over enabled `RadarTerrainObstacle` meshes in the active scene, at cruise height, with isolated authoring output buffers. Decorative rubble is excluded. Fail rather than publish partial geometry when the union fails. The texture clips to map extent, preserves aspect ratio (1024 pixels on its longest side), and has a muted sand grid, filled solid sections and outer edges. No interior overlap seams or invented route overlays. A scene-derived airfield position and heading are stored separately; the preview draws a runway glyph, not the complete airfield footprint.

Output: `Assets/_Project/Generated/MissionMaps/<scene>_<guid8>.asset` with a texture subasset, scene GUID, bounds, altitude and static anchor. Re-bake updates the existing asset, retaining references. `MissionMapData.WorldToUV` / `UVToWorld` provide the common un-clamped world X/Z mapping; +Z is north/up. The raster is a tactical obstruction map, not a heightmap or rendered overhead photograph. It is independent of the camera and runtime radar cache.

MAP-02 will bind the baked asset to the in-game map view and add M toggle, pause policy, current position/heading, objective/status and threat overlays. Do not duplicate planned SAM radii or marker coordinates in the UI. Mission boundaries remain a separate gameplay atom. No gameplay change in MAP-01. Unity compile, actual import, preview and rebake must be verified locally.
