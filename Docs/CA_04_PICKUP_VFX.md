# CA04 — Carryall pickup presentation

Accepted 2026-11-26 through CA04C. The ground wash is subtle and not strongly visible at game zoom, but accepted for now; further dust polish is deferred. Next focus is [CA05 destruction](CA_05_DESTRUCTION_PREVIEW.md).

Requires the accepted CA03 preview. Existing M02 airfield-side preview, Start Airborne, R and Tab are unchanged; no menu or scene edits. Press Play. Observe the six phases from the same camera or Scene view. CA01/CG01 prefab assets, accepted meshes/materials, rotor visual, pose timing, capture logic and real C1/C2 instances are untouched.

`CarryallPickupVfx` is a disposable presentation helper owned by the scene preview. Four tapered cool-blue/white downwash plumes attach to the authored downward exhaust sockets. Thrust is smoothed by pickup phase; descent is restrained, loaded lift is strongest, departure stays strong. Plume length is limited by the sampled terrain. One shared ground wash is active below 32 units of terrain clearance, strongest near the surface. Particles spawn in a broad annulus, spread radially in world space and fade; horizontal billboards keep the effect low to the ground. Clamp pulses keep their separate camera-facing particle system. Four small warm clamp puffs fire once when the successful load transition is observed, not continuously during Clamp. This is stylized visual feedback, not physical thrust or collision. No audio, new shaders, imported textures, lights, physics or damage. Uses the existing SandboxEffect shader; missing FX setup warns without blocking pickup.

Inspector on `M02 / CA01 flight preview`: Exhaust Scale (1, 0 disables), Dust Puffs Per Second (7 as density knob, 56/s total at full proximity before thrust multiplier, 0 disables), Clamp Pulse (on). Tunable live. R clears particles, returns visual thrust and phase to initial state. Tab uses existing mission pause and scaled time. Generated meshes, materials, masks and particle systems are instance-local and disposed on destruction; original art assets retain GUIDs.

Local acceptance: compile, play near the airport, check all four exhausts point down, no terrain clipping, clear rise in thrust after attachment, dust only near ground, one restrained clamp pulse, no duplicate effects on repeated R and no movement during Tab. Check Scene view and actual game camera at Y78. Reduce scale/density if noisy. No Unity Play Mode validation in the patch environment. Next atom: destroy an empty and a loaded Carryall in the same test area (including payload outcome), then integrate real C1/C2 evacuation separately.

CA04B visibility pass: larger 5.5–8.5 unit dust puffs, 1.1–1.7 s lifetime, stronger contrast, wider outward spawn and drift, 256 particle capacity and explicit position/color/UV billboard streams for SandboxEffect. Dust stays terrain-bound; the downwash meshes and clamp pulse tuning are unchanged. Visual acceptance remains local.

CA04C replaces the four individual dust sources with one unified low ground wash. Spawn radius 6–16, horizontal drift 5–9 units/s, size 12–19, life 1.5–2.2 s; softer alpha, ochre tint and a procedural irregular mask help overlapping particles read as one cloud. Dust samples terrain at spawn, not along the entire subsequent drift (flat airport preview only; steep slopes need later validation). Exhaust and clamp feedback unchanged.
