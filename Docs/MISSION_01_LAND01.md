# LAND-01 — assisted runway landing v0

Open Mission01_FirstSortie and Play. The launch sequence's Editor-only `debugLandingNearAirfield` defaults on: a cyan corridor appears within 550 units of the runway start even before recon is complete. Disable this to test normal gating; builds always require three Watchers destroyed. Optional objective is not required.

Approach from the takeoff side of the runway, facing opposite the takeoff heading (inbound toward the original spawn). The corridor spans 150–350 units along the takeoff axis, width 90, at cruise altitude. Align within 35 degrees, hold S for .8 seconds while inside. Fuel does not gate landing. The cue changes color and shows hold progress. Leaving the corridor or releasing S before capture resets progress.

After capture, autopilot owns the pose, disables weapons and SAM acquisition, removes existing mission SAM projectiles without resetting site HP/counters, blends from the current visual bank, descends to a touchdown 65 units ahead of the original runway spawn, and brakes to the original spawn at zero speed. The plane faces the opposite direction from takeoff. XZ cubic control points are constrained to the operation rectangle. The authored final approach must remain clear of terrain; this v0 trusts the runway corridor and does not simulate terrain impacts after capture. No landing gear art, audio, final result/debriefing or mission statistics yet. A debug capture before objective completion shows LANDING TEST COMPLETE, not MISSION COMPLETE.

Test: normal and debug air start, approach at both lateral edges and heading tolerance, release S before capture, empty boost tank, Tab pause/resume, R during approach/landing/after landing, and confirm touchdown on pavement. Inspect the reverse approach relative to the actual saved runway and terrain, tune fields if necessary. Unity compilation and Play Mode are local acceptance gates.

## LAND-01A — runway-width capture trial

The corridor now uses the actual Mission01 pavement runway centerline, inbound threshold and width (24 units before any scene scaling). The 200-unit approach extends outward from the threshold; the same rectangle is used for capture and ground cue. A small diamond indicates the aircraft's XZ ground projection, not its shadow. The new `landingCorridorWidthScale` defaults to 1 (runway width); 1.5 or 2 can be tested in the Inspector without widening the pavement. If the expected mesh or runway alignment cannot be resolved, log a warning and fall back to the takeoff axis, configured approach offset and 24-unit width. The .8-second hold, ±35° heading tolerance, objective/debug gating and autopilot timing formula remain unchanged. Validate the scene transform, cue/marker visibility and narrow approach in Play Mode.

## LAND-02 — hands-free capture and quicker flare

Supersedes the S-hold and timing rules above. Stay inside the runway-width corridor, aligned within the existing heading tolerance, for one continuous second. No input button or fuel is required. Leaving the corridor, losing alignment or focus, or opening Tab clears capture progress. Debug availability and mission completion gating are unchanged.

The approach cubic preserves captured horizontal velocity and touchdown tangent, but caps duration at three seconds. Default touchdown speed is 65 units/s and the existing 65-unit rollout stops in two seconds. Nose-up visual pitch blends from the current bank, increases from 3 to 10 degrees during flare, and returns to level over .65 seconds after touchdown. A small root-height offset imitates rear contact without gear geometry. No new audio or landing gear art. `landingCaptureSeconds` and `landingAutoTouchdownSpeed` intentionally replace the old serialized fields so saved LAND-01 defaults do not survive the change. Tune in Inspector after testing. Verify hands-free capture, reset on departure/misalignment, empty fuel, no touchdown pose jump, R/Tab, and clearance over the runway; the fake contact pivot is visual tuning, not physical landing gear.
