# CM-02 — Interception playground

Base: `da86980`. CM01 Sparrow visual approved. The prefab now owns a passive air-route motor, body-sized box hitbox and 1 HP air damage receiver with an aircraft radar contact. No AAM lock, guidance, evasion, weapons, or cruise smoke on the target.

Open ArcadeDesertE1. Interception Test on Air01Encounter overrides Ground Nurs Test; the scene enables it for this trial. AC-01 on LMB only. Ten targets at speed 55, spaced by 0.7 seconds, fly a straight test lane at the player's combat altitude. Each valid cannon hit removes a target and emits existing lethal-hit particles. Arrival at the end counts as escaped. HUD shows destroyed/total, escaped, and live targets; no victory/defeat rule. R or boundary reset clears the wave and restarts at player spawn. Altitude test keys keep targets in the same plane. Turn Interception Test off to return to the previous ground/air tests.

Tune count and interval on Air01Encounter and speed/HP on CM01_Sparrow. The straight route is deliberately test-only; AirRouteMotor supports a list of points, with visually smoothed heading at waypoints. No general mission system or enemy spawn architecture introduced. Playtest: hit body, confirm radar removal, watch escaped count, reset mid-wave and after completion, then verify both previous modes.
