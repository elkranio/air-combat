# CM-02 — Interception playground

Base: `da86980`. CM01 Sparrow visual approved. The prefab now owns a passive air-route motor, body-sized box hitbox and 1 HP air damage receiver with an aircraft radar contact. The target has no active guidance, evasion, weapons, or cruise smoke. CM-03 adds player AAM designation through the common AirDamageableTarget contract.

Open ArcadeDesertE1. Interception Test on Air01Encounter overrides Ground Nurs Test; the scene enables it for this trial. AC-01 on LMB and the existing AAM on RMB. Ten targets at speed 55, spaced by 0.7 seconds, fly individually offset straight test lanes at the player's combat altitude. Each lethal cannon or AAM hit removes a target and emits existing lethal-hit particles. Arrival at the end counts as escaped. HUD shows destroyed/total, escaped, and live targets; no victory/defeat rule. R clears the wave and restarts at player spawn. Automatic player boundary reset is disabled for free-flight terrain testing; the encounter still uses its configured bounds for enemy routes and spawning. Altitude test keys keep targets in the same plane. Turn Interception Test off to return to the previous ground/air tests.

Tune count, interval and Interception Lateral Spread (default 60 world units on either side of the original lane; zero restores the single lane) on Air01Encounter and speed/HP on CM01_Sparrow. The straight route is deliberately test-only; AirRouteMotor supports a list of points, with visually smoothed heading at waypoints. No general mission system or enemy spawn architecture introduced. Playtest: hit with AC-01, then hover for the existing AAM lock and fire RMB; verify destruction is counted once, radar and designation removal on kill or escape, two-shot magazine and existing reload, reset mid-wave and after completion, then verify both previous modes. Audio design deferred.

## CM-04 — Flight personality (trial)

CM-03 cannon and AAM interception were confirmed in Play Mode by the player. CM-04 adds smooth lateral sway and visual roll to Sparrow, without changing the nominal route progress, altitude, HP, spawn timing or weapon settings. On `CM01_Sparrow > AirRouteMotor > Flight Personality`, tune Sway Amplitude (2.5 world units max), Sway Frequency (0.65 Hz nominal), and Roll Amplitude (10 degrees max). Per-spawn amplitude and roll are 60–100% of the settings; frequency is 75–125% with a random starting phase. Zero frequency disables the motion; zero amplitudes disable the respective channels. The root carries position and hitbox; VisualRoot carries cosmetic roll and the exhaust socket. Check individual motion, cannon/AAM hits, radar, altitude changes, escape counts and R reset. No active evasion or new audio.
