# CA05 — Carryall destruction visual trial

**Status (2026-11-26):** committed prototype, not visually accepted. Resume with the user's animation feedback next session (specific notes not yet supplied). Both unloaded and loaded destruction must be accepted before integration into real C1/C2. The intact landed cargo is a test outcome, not a final recovery/scoring rule. See [handoff](../HANDOFF.md).

Requires CA04C. Keep the existing airfield-side CA01 preview. No new scene menu, prefab change, target, damage receiver, radar contact, reward or mission condition. Start Airborne remains optional. Play, K triggers loss at the current pose, L cycles Pickup → Empty hover → Loaded hover → Pickup and resets only this preview; R resets the sortie and restores the selected preview mode. Tab pauses the sequence. Test Mode is an Inspector start/retry choice. Forced hover uses the pad XZ at cruise altitude for quick repeated tests. In Pickup mode K uses the actual attachment state; a cargo that is still on the ground is unaffected.

`CarryallCrashSequence` takes over pose updates from the pickup sequence on K, immediately clears downwash, spins the four rotors down and emits a small flash and smoke trail from one engine. The hull drifts, loses altitude and banks progressively before a terrain contact. Approximate contact uses transformed local mesh-bound corners (flat test terrain, not physics collision or terrain routing). The existing GroundCrashFeedback supplies the impact fire, dust, smoke, sparks and fragments, with no damage, camera shake or sound. The wreck remains with instance-only charred paint until reset; no shared material changes. No separated nacelle meshes or deforming hull yet.

Loaded kill immediately detaches the CG01 at its current world pose. It falls separately with inherited horizontal movement, small separation drift and slightly stronger stylized downward acceleration; remains upright and intact after contact, with a small dust puff. No gameplay recovery, cargo damage, secondary explosion or collision between hull and cargo yet. R clears all effects, restores original material property blocks, resets the rotor phase, and reparents/places the same cargo without clones. Defaults: crash acceleration 24 units/s², minimum horizontal drift 16 units/s. K does nothing once crash is active.

Local acceptance: check K during Pickup before attachment and during loaded lift/depart; L to Empty, K, R, repeat; L to Loaded, K, R, repeat; L back to Pickup. Verify R mid-fall and after landing, no duplicate cargo/rotors, no changed shared materials, no residual particles, Tab pause. Confirm real C1/C2 and convoy untouched. Airborne/terrain contact is approximate and the intact charred hull is a placeholder wreck visual. Unity compilation and actual camera acceptance remain local.
