# CA05 — Carryall destruction visual trial

**Status (2026-11-27):** CA05 hull crash accepted as good enough; further hull animation polish deferred. CA05B adds cargo breakup, pending visual acceptance. Test both variants before actual weapon interaction and integration into C1/C2. See [handoff](../HANDOFF.md).

Requires CA04C. Keep the existing airfield-side CA01 preview. No new scene menu, prefab change, target, damage receiver, radar contact, reward or mission condition. Start Airborne remains optional. Play, K triggers loss at the current pose, L cycles Pickup → Empty hover → Loaded hover → Pickup and resets only this preview; R resets the sortie and restores the selected preview mode. Tab pauses the sequence. Test Mode is an Inspector start/retry choice. Forced hover uses the pad XZ at cruise altitude for quick repeated tests. In Pickup mode K uses the actual attachment state; a cargo that is still on the ground is unaffected.

`CarryallCrashSequence` takes over pose updates from the pickup sequence on K, immediately clears downwash, spins the four rotors down and emits a small flash and smoke trail from one engine. The hull drifts, loses altitude and banks progressively before a terrain contact. Approximate contact uses transformed local mesh-bound corners (flat test terrain, not physics collision or terrain routing). The existing GroundCrashFeedback supplies the impact fire, dust, smoke, sparks and fragments, with no damage, camera shake or sound. The wreck remains with instance-only charred paint until reset; no shared material changes. No separated nacelle meshes or deforming hull yet.

Loaded kill immediately detaches CG01 at its current world pose. It falls separately with inherited horizontal movement, small separation drift and slightly stronger stylized downward acceleration, now gradually tipping (12 degrees pitch, 24 degrees roll). At terrain impact CA05B hides its original Visual subtree and scatters temporary mesh-only copies of the 13 authored assemblies. Cradle and core stay heavy, larger panels separate, lighter ribs and capture fittings tumble; a restrained flash and dust accompany contact. Pieces settle against terrain using their transformed mesh bounds, shrink after 4.5 s and disappear at 6 s. No rigidbodies, shared asset edits, gameplay damage, recovery, or hull/cargo collision. R clears all effects and copies, restores original cargo visibility and hull material property blocks, resets the rotor phase, and reparents/places the same cargo. Defaults: crash acceleration 24 units/s², minimum horizontal drift 16 units/s. K does nothing once crash is active.

Local acceptance: check K during Pickup before attachment and during loaded lift/depart; L to Empty, K, R, repeat; L to Loaded, K, R, repeat; L back to Pickup. Verify R mid-fall, during breakup and after debris cleanup; cargo remains intact when killed before attachment. Check all assemblies scatter once, no duplicate cargo/rotors, no changed shared materials, no residual particles, Tab pause. Confirm real C1/C2 and convoy untouched. Airborne/terrain contact is approximate and the intact charred hull is a placeholder wreck visual. Unity compilation and actual camera acceptance remain local.

Next: an opt-in stationary firing trial at the airfield. Freeze the player at a position and heading from which the existing AC01 and NURS can hit the test area without flying another attack pass. Hold CA01 at cruise height above the cargo, command descent manually, and allow repeated tests before attachment, at Clamp, early Lift and near the cannon flight plane. Preserve real projectile trajectories, test exact hitbox/range overlap and actual damage acceptance. Unattached cargo destroyed: abort pickup and climb/depart empty.
