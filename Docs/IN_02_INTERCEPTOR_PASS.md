# IN02 — Interceptor one-pass sandbox

Existing IN01 V2 model and original materials; no modeling, prefab, M01/M02 or Tiger balance edits. CombatSandbox selects new enum value 5 (Interceptor) and hides the IN01 art comparison and ground combat actors at runtime only. Other mode IDs retain their meanings. The existing Kestrel combat player is kept (same 70 cruise / 140 boost tuning as Lynx); AC01 and player AAM are equipped, NURS off. Start/R set a deterministic airborne head-on geometry: player (0,-300) facing north and enemy (0,+300) facing south, existing altitude Y78. R resets health, ammo, boost, actors and warnings. Switch mode before Play to return to prior tests.

Pilot is limited to 30 deg/s while steering toward the current player position. It requires a 110–300 range and 45-degree forward cone to commit to the two-shot salvo. Existing warning precedes the first launch; shots 0.38 s apart and each leaves its actual model hardpoint, then hides that prop. Once acquisition begins both shots are committed, even if the player changes course; missile pursuit still has its finite turn budget. A pass that fails to acquire by timeout/close crossing/near boundary breaks away empty. Breakaway toward north slows from 96 to 55 u/s with 32 deg/s yaw and at least 2.2 seconds in the breakaway phase, then accelerates to 125 u/s. The existing player weapons can damage IN01 in every airborne phase (5 HP). A kill removes pending launch, not live missiles. Departure beyond the sandbox safe edge removes only the aircraft; the test waits for R. The two shots use the existing hostile AAM flight, warning, damage and radar pipeline with isolated settings (180 speed / 70 deg/s / 100 deg budget / 5 damage). No simulated rearm, airfield or second sortie in this atom.

Local gates: Play CombatSandbox; fly straight first, then R and turn left/right during approach, boost, S-turn; observe lock/no-lock, two distinct launches and return. Test AC01 and AAM before, during and after breakaway; kill after shot one; ensure remaining shot is cancelled but the launched missile remains dangerous. R mid-warning, mid-salvo, after escape and after player death; check radar, target markers, missile props, HP and boost. Keep altitude fixed during each pass. Try old Dogfight and Convoy modes before Play; no change in their tuning. The angle/speed/shot window values are hypotheses for tuning. Unity compilation and in-game feel still require local validation.
