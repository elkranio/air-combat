# CA03 — Carryall cargo pickup visual trial

Requires the accepted CA02 preview. No new scene installer or prefab edits. In the saved M02 scene keep `M02 / CA01 flight preview` near the airfield (if absent, install via the existing CA02 menu). Press Play; enable Start Airborne on Mission02RouteTrial for fast flybys. The ground pickup is directly under the preview root XZ, using the scene terrain surface plus 0.15 clearance. Observe the whole sequence in Scene view while playing if the moving player camera leaves the area. R resets the sequence and the sortie; Tab pauses it. The existing CA02 removal menu still removes the whole preview.

`CarryallFlightPreview` keeps the serialized carrier/payload links and rotor controller; the old circular path and Show Payload toggle are replaced by the pickup controller. On Play the test CG01 is detached from CargoMount and placed on the ground, not one of the real C1/C2 cargo instances. A separate reusable `CarryallPickupSequence` owns pose interpolation, phase and payload parenting. It takes explicit actors, ground pose and tuning, has no scene lookup or mission dependencies. The four world-space capture points and the mount pose are checked before attachment (0.02 units / 0.1 degrees). Clamp is a short hold, not a mechanical jaw animation. Loaded cargo is a rigid child of CargoMount; there is no physics sway. Geometry, material assets, sockets and accepted rotor visuals remain unchanged.

Defaults (Inspector on the existing preview root, R snapshots timing edits): Approach 3.5 s from 56 units away and 14 lateral offset; Align 1.2 s; Descend 5 s to capture plane; Clamp 1.1 s; loaded Lift 6.5 s to Y78; Depart 9 s accelerating over 150 units. Total 26.3 s. At Complete it holds the far endpoint for inspection until R; not actual mission extraction. Rotors use 720 degrees/s. Phase Status is diagnostic. Approach/align/descend/lift ease in/out, loaded lift is slower than descent. No new hover/bank while aligning to ensure the sockets line up. Ground clearance, altitude, distances, times and rotor speed are tunable. No deadline, damage, objectives or radar changes. CA04 adds instance-only presentation; see [CA04](CA_04_PICKUP_VFX.md). Real pickup spots and routes will be authored separately.

Local gate: compile, open M02, Play, verify cargo stays grounded until Clamp, exact contact without a snap, loaded lift and departure. R during approach, clamp, lift and completion should restore the ground cargo and empty carrier; verify no clones or duplicate rotor meshes. Test Tab pause and return. If the terrain-only test pad intersects pavement or decoration, move the existing preview root over clear ground near the airport; no automatic collision routing. Confirm normal convoy behavior and both real cargo locations unchanged. Unity Play Mode is required for acceptance.
