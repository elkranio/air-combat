BD04B layout accepted; this atom does not move buildings or change routes. Open and save M02, run **Air Combat > Missions > M02 - Enable BD05 Picket SAM**. No prefab meshes or health settings are edited. `Picket Sam Enabled`, `Base Deployment Trial`, `Base Trial Hold Aircraft` apply on R. Turn Hold Aircraft on for a static target; turn SAM off to observe all deployment animations without taking damage. Full sortie start is restored by disabling Base Deployment Trial; SAM stays enabled if desired.

The existing SurfaceToAirLauncher copies acquisition, warning, reload, projectile and range from the stationary Picket template (currently 430 range, 2s lock, 5s reload, 0.65s warning). Existing prelaunch HUD and radar missile contacts are reused. No arming during march. LateUpdate continues checking arming even after the convoy movement clock stops.

R and failure cancel live owned shots; detached spent trails and existing impact effects retain their normal self-expiry. The stationary and sandbox variants keep their prior rules. Diagnostic HUD adds armed count and total launches.
